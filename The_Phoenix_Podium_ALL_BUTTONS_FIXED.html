Debating Tips

Structure your speech

Use an opening, main points, evidence, rebuttal and a conclusion.

Use evidence

Support your ideas with examples, facts, case studies or expert opinions.

Explain your point

Do not just state an opinion. Explain why it matters and who it affects.

Listen carefully

Good rebuttal starts with careful listening. Write down the opponent's main points.

Use signposting

Say phrases like “my first point is” and “therefore” to guide the audience.

End strongly

Finish by reminding people why your side is more convincing overall.
