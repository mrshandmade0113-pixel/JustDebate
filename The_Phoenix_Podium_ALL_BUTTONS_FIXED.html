Debating Tips

Use these simple tips to make your speeches clearer, stronger and more persuasive.

1

Structure your speech

Use an opening, main points, evidence, rebuttal and a clear conclusion.

2

Use evidence

Support your ideas with examples, facts, statistics or real-life cases.

3

Explain your point

Do not just state an opinion. Explain why it matters and who it affects.

4

Listen carefully

Write down your opponent’s key points so you can respond properly.

5

Use signposting

Say phrases like “my first point is” and “therefore” to guide your audience.

6

End strongly

Finish by reminding people why your side is more convincing.

School and Education

For: Uniforms can reduce clothing pressure and create a sense of belonging.
Against: Uniforms may limit self-expression and cost families money.
